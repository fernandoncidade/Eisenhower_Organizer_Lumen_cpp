SQLite Blessing / Public Domain Notice

SQLite is commonly dedicated to the public domain by its authors. The SQLite
project publishes a short blessing for users who need a written dedication:

May you do good and not evil.
May you find forgiveness for yourself and forgive others.
May you share freely, never taking more than you give.

For authoritative and current SQLite copyright information, consult:
https://sqlite.org/copyright.html
