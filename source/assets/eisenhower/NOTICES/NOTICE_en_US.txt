Statement on data transmission and privacy
- The standard executable does not send automatic telemetry to external servers.
- User data, such as tasks and exported files, remains stored locally under the user's control.
- For further details, see the Privacy Policy included with the distribution.
- When the Feynman module is active, concepts, explanations, reviews, and linked-file paths remain in the user's local environment, except for any external action performed by the user.
- When the Mind Map module is active, documents chosen by the user for import are read for local extraction/analysis, and maps/autosaves remain in the user's local environment except for any external action performed by the user.
- When the Audiobook module is active, texts and PDFs remain local during editing/reading; however, when an external-service voice such as Edge TTS is selected and reading is started, the selected text may be transmitted to the voice provider to generate audio.
