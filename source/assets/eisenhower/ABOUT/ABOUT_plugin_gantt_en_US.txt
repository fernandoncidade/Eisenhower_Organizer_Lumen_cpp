Description:
The Gantt & 4D BIM module adds advanced project planning, schedule management, an interactive Gantt chart, the Critical Path Method (CPM), and 4D simulation to Eisenhower Organizer. It works as supplementary functionality integrated into the parent application.

Main features:
- Interactive Gantt chart with support for dependencies (FS, SS, FF, SF) and milestones.
- Automatic Critical Path Method (CPM) calculation, float analysis, and early/late dates.
- Hierarchical WBS (Work Breakdown Structure) table with deadlines, costs, progress, and statuses.
- Baseline management and Ghost Line overlay for schedule comparison.
- 4D construction simulation synchronized with timeline progress.
- Schedule import and export via standard CSV format.
- Local bidirectional synchronization with Eisenhower Organizer tasks and priorities.

Data and connectivity:
- The module operates locally and does not transmit telemetry to the developer.
- Schedules, task relationships, baselines, simulations, and CSV files remain on the user's local device.
