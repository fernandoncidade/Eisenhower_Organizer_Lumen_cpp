Short description:
EISENHOWER ORGANIZER is a native Windows application that helps users organize tasks with the Eisenhower Matrix, separating activities by importance, urgency, dates, times, status, and priorities. This document describes only the parent application. Integrated modules, such as Pomodoro, Feynman, Mind Map, Audiobook, Gantt & BIM 4D, Assessment Agenda, and Local Qwen AI Model, have their own documents.

Main features:
- Task creation, editing, completion, and removal.
- Organization into four quadrants: Important and Urgent; Important but Not Urgent; Not Important but Urgent; Not Important and Not Urgent.
- Separate lists for pending and completed tasks.
- Calendar, date-based views, and upcoming task panel.
- Local data persistence in the user profile.
- Import and export in supported formats, depending on the resources available in the package.
- Brazilian Portuguese and U.S. English interface.
- About window with policies, licenses, notices, and support information.

Installation and distribution:

Privacy and data:

- Tasks, preferences, and exported files remain on the device or in the location chosen by the user.
