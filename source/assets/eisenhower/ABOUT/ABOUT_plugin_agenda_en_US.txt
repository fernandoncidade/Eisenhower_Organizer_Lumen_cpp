Module: Assessment Agenda & Activity Tracker
Product ID: modulo_agenda
Version: 2026.11.22.0
Author: Fernando Nillsson Cidade

Description:
The integrated Assessment Agenda & Activity Tracker module brings comprehensive chronological planning, logging, and tracking of assessments, exams, deadlines, and academic/professional commitments to EISENHOWER ORGANIZER. It runs as a native built-in component of the main application.

Key features:
- Registration, editing, prioritization, and deletion of activities and assessments.
- Organization by dates, times, categories, weights, grades, and completion status.
- Chronological list view and integration with calendar view.
- Structured local persistence in a local SQLite database within the user's profile.
- Local bidirectional synchronization with the main Eisenhower Matrix tasks and priorities.
- Activity export and import for local archiving and reporting.

Data and connectivity:
- The module operates 100% locally on the device.
- All database tables, records, and preferences remain strictly under user control in the local database file.
- No agenda details, commitments, or grades are transmitted to external servers.
