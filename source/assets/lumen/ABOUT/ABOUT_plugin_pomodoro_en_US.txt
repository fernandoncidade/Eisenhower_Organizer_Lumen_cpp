Description:
The Pomodoro module adds a focus module to LÚMEN with work and break cycles, local task tracking, and alarm configuration. It works as a native module integrated into the main application.

Main features:
- Focus and break cycles.
- Local workflow for pending, in-progress, and completed tasks.
- Local integration with parent app tasks.
- Alarm sound and repeat settings.
- Locally stored preferences.
