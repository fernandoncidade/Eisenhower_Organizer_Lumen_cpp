Description:
The Audiobook module adds an accessible reader for texts and PDFs, with aloud reading, search, navigation, visual adjustments, and playback controls. It works as a native module integrated into the main application.

Main features:
- Opening, editing, search, and reading of texts.
- Reading and navigation of PDFs chosen by the user.
- Voice, speed, volume, and font selection.
- Speech highlighting and focus ruler.
- Temporary audio files during synthesis.

Data and connectivity:
- The module operates locally by default.
- Online voices, such as Edge TTS, may require sending the selected text to the voice provider when the user starts reading.
- Purchase, license, and access confirmation are processed by the Microsoft Store.
