  - Text and date/time formats are reapplied when changing language without requiring restart.

  - Small style improvements ensure a consistent look across modules.

  - A list of available sounds can be chosen with quick preview.
  - Sound preference and repeat interval are saved between sessions.
  - Repetitive alarms behave predictably: audio plays, waits (sound duration + configured interval) and repeats until stopped.
  - The stop action interrupts playback and repetition immediately; previewing does not change the persisted selection.
  - Fallbacks and invalid-file handling ensure safe behavior even when a sound is missing or corrupted.

Robustness and logs
- Multiple protections were added to prevent painting, locale, palette or audio errors from freezing the UI.
- Logs were expanded to ease diagnosis of issues without impacting the user experience.

Quick checks

   - Switch between Day/Week/Month and confirm the list is filtered/ordered.
3. Change language and confirm texts and date/time formats update.
4. Switch theme (light/dark) and check lists and dropdowns do not show artifacts.
5. Test sound:
   - Open sound settings, preview a sound, save the selection and start a repeating alarm; confirm it repeats at the expected interval and stopping halts the cycle.
   - Test behavior with a missing or invalid sound to confirm safe fallback.

What changes for you
- A more informative calendar that visually matches the app theme.
- Better experience when switching language and theme.
- More predictable sound alarms, with preview, saved preference and controlled repetition until confirmation.
